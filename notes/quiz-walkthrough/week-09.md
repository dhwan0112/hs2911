### Q1 — Longstreet & Brooks internet addiction definition  ·  Answer: B
**Q:** Which description of internet addiction (Longstreet & Brooks, 2017) is correct?
**Why B:** Impulse control disorder: dependence on the internet for psychological wellbeing; deprivation brings heightened unpleasant feelings. `[S2]`
**Why not the others:** A) not a normal developmental stage · C) about social contact, not internet · D) not a mood disorder with depression as sole cause · E) not defined by hours alone
**Trap:** wrong construct

### Q2 — Social media vs internet addiction relationship  ·  Answer: E
**Q:** How is social media addiction related to internet addiction (PIU)?
**Why E:** Social media addiction is a subset of internet addiction focused on habitual problematic use of social media platforms. `[S2]`
**Why not the others:** A) reversed subset · B) reversed, and gaming is invented · C) not interchangeable · D) "replaced as a diagnosis" is not on the slide
**Trap:** reversed direction

### Q3 — Intense vs problematic use  ·  Answer: D
**Q:** Which statement correctly separates intense from problematic use (Boer et al., 2020)?
**Why D:** Intense = duration and frequency; problematic = preoccupation and lack of control; distinct but correlated. `[S3]`
**Why not the others:** A) definitions swapped · B) not clinical vs lay, not unrelated · C) wrong definitions and "unrelated" · E) both are not just time online
**Trap:** swapped definitions

### Q4 — DSM-5 status of internet addictions  ·  Answer: A
**Q:** What is the status of internet-related addictions in the DSM-5?
**Why A:** IGD is the only form of internet addiction currently included; its criteria serve as the diagnostic reference (Besser et al., 2019). `[S3]`
**Why not the others:** B) no single all-internet diagnosis · C) social media addiction is not listed · D) IGD is included · E) social media addiction is not a formal diagnosis
**Trap:** overstated inclusion

### Q5 — Three functions and two motivations  ·  Answer: D
**Q:** Which pairing of three functions and two motivations is correct?
**Why D:** Functions: social interactions, community inputs, content sharing. Motivations: belongingness, self-presentation. `[S5]`
**Why not the others:** A) mixes sub-items and invents "motivations" · B) mixes lists · C) sub-items (entertainment, education, networking) as functions · E) functions and motivations swapped
**Trap:** list mixing / swapped levels

### Q6 — Match four models to their focus  ·  Answer: D
**Q:** Which option matches each model (Ho et al., 2017) to its focus?
**Why D:** CB = maladaptation in cognitive processes; social skill = deficits in social interaction; social cognitive = deficiency in self-regulation; TPB = attitude and norms. `[S6]`
**Why not the others:** A) foci rotated across models · B) foci shuffled · C) foci shuffled · E) foci shuffled, and TPB given self-regulation
**Trap:** shuffled pairings

### Q7 — Life-satisfaction chain direction  ·  Answer: B
**Q:** Which chain links emotional states to addiction via life satisfaction (Longstreet & Brooks, 2017)?
**Why B:** More stress -> lower life satisfaction -> more addiction; more happiness -> higher life satisfaction -> less addiction. `[S7]`
**Why not the others:** A) addiction-to-stress direction · C) happiness/stress effects on satisfaction reversed · D) happiness raising addiction · E) invented stress-only moderation
**Trap:** reversed direction

### Q8 — 84.7% vs 29.5% figures  ·  Answer: E
**Q:** What do 84.7% (active use, 2023) and 29.5% (college students addicted, 2017) show?
**Why E:** They measure different things in different groups and years; 29.5% is not the share of users who are addicted. `[S2]`
**Why not the others:** A) 84.7% is the whole population · B) not the same behaviour/group · C) not a time series · D) 29.5% is not a subset of the 84.7%
**Trap:** number out of context

### Q9 — Addicted brain pattern  ·  Answer: A
**Q:** Which pattern describes the addicted state versus the balanced brain?

**Why not the others:** B) memories become stronger, not weaker · C) hippocampus/amygdala still influence · D) top-down weakens, "NO-GO" is balanced state · E) pathway does not become inactive
**Trap:** reversed direction

### Q12 — Montag & Markett FoMO, SNUD, failures  ·  Answer: E
