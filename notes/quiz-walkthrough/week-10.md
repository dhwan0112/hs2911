### Q1 — Waterman definition of psychological well-being  ·  Answer: E
**Q:** Which definition matches Waterman et al. (2010) for psychological well-being?
**Why E:** The extent to which an individual can live meaningfully according to their deeply held values. `[S4]`
**Why not the others:** A) life satisfaction by income/health/relationships, invented · B) absence of disorder plus stable mood, invented · C) WHO mental-health wording (flourish, potential, contribute) · D) coping/recovery is resilience (S14), not well-being
**Trap:** right wording, wrong construct (WHO mental health vs Waterman well-being). Note: rationale says "the first wrong option" is the WHO definition, but it is actually option C.

### Q2 — Key message of evolving social media landscape  ·  Answer: D
**Q:** Algorithm feeds, infinite scrolling, notifications, short videos, recommendation systems: key message about young people?

**Why not the others:** A) advertising/revenue claim, not on slide · B) says they search via friends; opposite · C) says they curate feeds, opposite of passive exposure · E) less time spent, not claimed
